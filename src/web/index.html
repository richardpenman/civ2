OpenCiv2

Civilization II Multiplayer Gold Edition, rebuilt to run in your browser. It plays with your own copy of the game: choose the folder Civilization II is installed in (the one with Civ2Art.dll and RULES.TXT), the game's CD, or a disc image of the CD. The files are copied into this browser's storage, and never leave your computer; your saved games are kept there too.

or drop the folder, the .zip, or the .cue with its .bin here

From the CD (the folder it is in, with DATA1.CAB and CIV2) or a disc image of it, the game is unpacked as its installer would, with its videos; from a disc image its music too, encoded as Ogg Vorbis (a few minutes; the browser cannot read a CD's music tracks, a disc image has them). Music plays if the game's folder has it (Music/Track02.ogg ..., as the set-up makes it). Single player only: network games are not available in the browser.

The game files are in this browser.
