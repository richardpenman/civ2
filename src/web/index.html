OpenCiv2

Civilization II Multiplayer Gold Edition, rebuilt to run in your browser. It plays with your own copy of the game: the folder Civilization II is installed in (the one with Civ2Art.dll and RULES.TXT), the game's CD, or a disc image of the CD. How to play (Standard or Classic, HD graphics, the rule changes and the interface additions) is chosen in the game: OpenCiv2 Options, in its main menu.
