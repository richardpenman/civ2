# HD graphics (docs/HD-GRAPHICS.md): the map redrawn under a moving unit
# leaves no trail, gap or seam on the plane: at each of the 16 zooms (from
# the furthest out, zooming in step by step), a trireme, alone and stacked
# (its second shield reaches outside its sprite), slid from the map's
# centre in each of the 8 directions (hd_map_redraw_probe; after Peter
# Johnson's tools/test_map_redraw.py, PR #11).  The result is in hook.log:
# "map redraw probe: zoom Z: N of 16 slides left pixels" (0 at every zoom).
# The script waits for each probe (replay_hold).
# run: CIV2_TEST_HD=2 CIV2_TEST_FULL=1024x768 CIV2_HD_PACK=.../atlas-hd.pack
wait 8000
key Down Down Down Down     # "Load a Game"
wait 500

wait 20000

wait 8000
call menu_command 4e2803 0 804      # the furthest zoom out
