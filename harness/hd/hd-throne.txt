# HD graphics (docs/HD-GRAPHICS.md): the throne room drawn whole from the
# pack's art (src/game/hd_graphics.cpp, hd_throne_room) at several levels,
# and its choice screen, on the autosave (the Romans, civilization 1:
# arch 1, rug 1, dais 0, columns 1, tapestry 0, brazier 0, throne 2,
# ceiling medallion 0).  The room is improved 34 times
# (throneroom_show_upgrade), each section picked by a click in the region of
# the game's click map that chooses it (the same with HD graphics off: its
# logical screenshots and rand() trace are those of the run without them):
# every section to level 1 at least, then to 4 (a mixed room on the way),
# then the seven bonus pieces.  Shots: start (the room before), each
# improvement's room up-N (up-4: every section at level 1 at least, up-15:
# mixed, up-27: every section at 4, up-34: complete, all the bonus pieces),
# complete (the room looked at: throneroom_show); choices-first,
# choices-mixed, choices-bonus, choices-lastbonus (the choice screens).
# (The waits are long: the dissolves take minutes of the replay's clock, a
# click while the choices dissolve in is dropped, as by the original, and the
# shots want each room whole.  A room opened inside another no longer crashes
# the game: docs/BUGS.md, memory errors.)
# run: CIV2_TEST_HD=2 CIV2_TEST_FULL=640x480
