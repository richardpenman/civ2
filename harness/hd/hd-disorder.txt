# HD graphics (docs/HD-GRAPHICS.md): a city in civil disorder, its marker
# (the game's, in place of the size box) tinted in the owner's colour:
# Rome (36,60) of the autosave put in disorder and the map centred on it
# (hd_test_city_disorder, a test hook: address 0) at the zooms 0, 2, 4, -2
# and -4; then the Sioux' Slim Buttes (38,76): its marker in purple.
# run: CIV2_TEST_HD=2 CIV2_TEST_FULL=1024x768

wait 8000

shot d-4

shot d-m4
call hd_test_city_disorder 0 38 76 2    # Slim Buttes (near the map's edge: shown below Rome)
