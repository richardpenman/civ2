OpenCiv2: third-party software
==============================

The OpenCiv2 programs (the releases: OpenCiv2.exe, OpenCiv2, OpenCiv2.app)
contain the following software by others, under their licences, given in
full or as their notices require below.  The game itself (Civilization II)
is not included: it is set up from the player's own copy.

  1. unshield 1.6.2           MIT licence; its MD5 code: RSA's notice
  2. zlib 1.3.1               zlib licence
  3. libogg 1.3.5             BSD licence (3 clauses)
  4. libvorbis 1.3.7          BSD licence (3 clauses)
  5. stb_vorbis 1.22          MIT licence (or public domain)
  6. SDL 2                    zlib licence
  7. Indeo 4 decoder (FFmpeg) GNU LGPL 2.1 or later
  8. wasm2c runtime (wabt)    Apache License 2.0
  9. wasi-libc (wasi-sdk)     MIT licence (and others, see below)
 10. Liberation fonts 2.1.5   SIL Open Font License 1.1
 11. DejaVu fonts 2.37        Bitstream Vera licence (and public domain)

unshield, zlib (the set-up's reader of the CD's InstallShield cabinet),
libogg and libvorbis (its Ogg Vorbis encoder of the CD's music) are in the
program as their authors released them (tools/fetch-toolchain.sh); the
others as src/third_party and tools/build_port.py say.
